    MORE THAN A CAMPAIGN

    A journey toward awareness.

    Pink Journey is an interactive digital experience created by Open Minds Club for Octobre Rose, the breast cancer awareness campaign.

    Our mission is to make awareness more engaging, accessible, and memorable through interactive games, educational quizzes, and positive messages. Discover, learn, and contribute to a meaningful cause.

    Every small action matters.

    Awareness starts with knowledge, grows through conversation, and becomes stronger when we share it with others.

    Play. Learn. Share. Make your mark.

    Pink Journey is an educational awareness initiative, not a medical diagnostic tool. For health concerns, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

    How it works

    1. Choose your path. Pick any activity on the map.
    2. Play and learn. Games, quizzes and challenges.
    3. Earn points. Unlock achievements as you go.
    4. Make an impact. Finish 2 of 3 categories to complete the journey.
